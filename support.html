Frequently asked questions

How do I save a memory?
Open the Today tab, choose a photo, optionally add a short note, and tap "Save Good Day." You can edit or replace it any time from the Today tab or from Calendar.

Does Good Days work offline?
Yes. Photos and notes save to your device immediately, with no internet connection required. If you're signed in to iCloud, your memories also sync privately to your own iCloud account in the background whenever you're online.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Open Settings in the app and tap "Manage Subscription" — this opens Apple's subscription management. You can also go to your iPhone's Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. A one-time "Forever" purchase never renews, so there's nothing to manage or cancel.

How do I delete my data?
Good Days doesn't use accounts, so there's no account to delete. To remove everything, open Settings → Memories & data → "Delete All Memories." This permanently removes every memory from your device and, if you sync through iCloud, from your private iCloud database too.
